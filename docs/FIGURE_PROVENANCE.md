## Interpretation guardrails

- The pipeline and topology figures summarize implementation structure; they do not prove that the complete system was run from this cleaned repository.
- The PARS figure is evidence for one training/evaluation presentation in the manuscript. Multiple archived histories and conflicting training horizons remain unresolved, so the figure is not designated as the canonical experiment.
- The PARS figure does not establish a live SAC-to-Unity control handoff.
- Unity scene imagery selected for the README is published through `figures/unity_scene_and_robot.png`; additional scene screenshots remain local to avoid repetition.
